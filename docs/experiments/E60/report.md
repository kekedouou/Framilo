## Findings, separated by evidence strength

1. Installed Eagle main.js creates a visible BrowserWindow before loadURL, then sends plugin-create/run100ms after dom-ready. Pre-DOM/page-drawing delays can therefore appear as a wholly black window. This explains exposure of an empty host surface, not the duration or initiating fault. Read-only snippets in host-main.js.txt; no installation change or private IPC invocation.
2. Plugin prepareEntry waited for two requestAnimationFrame callbacks with no deadline. A suppressed callback deterministically prevents further initialization. Added a250ms timer escape, preserving the first opportunity to paint and cancellation on close. Model reproduces suppression and verifies release. Timers cannot solve a blocked renderer thread/driver, and this is not proven to be the user's10-second episode.
3. Native startup serially indexes video, indexes audio, hashes the whole source, opens reader, verifies/copies audio and awaits HTMLAudio readiness before the first video seek. Proxy generation starts earlier in parallel. These costs warrant separate instrumentation, but cannot alone explain an absent header/preparing message. Did not alter audio/decoder/cache policy speculatively.
